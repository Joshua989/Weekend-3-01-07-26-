Issues Reporting Outlet

One safe place for AAUA students and the indigenes of Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, Ayegunle and neighbouring communities to report issues that affect town–gown relations — and to follow them until they are resolved.

Report an Issue Track My Report

1. Report

Describe what happened, where and when. You can stay anonymous.

2. Get a ticket

You receive a ticket ID (e.g. IRO-26-4F7K2). Keep it safe.

3. Track

Use your ticket ID to see the status and any updates from the IRO desk.

4. Resolution

The IRO desk reviews, escalates to the right authority and records the outcome.

What can I report?

Emergency? IRO is not an emergency line. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact campus security or the nearest police division first, then file a report here.
